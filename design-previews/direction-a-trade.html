What we fix

Four things make up most of our work. If your problem isn't listed, call anyway — we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth repairing.

Computer repairs

Laptops, desktops, Macs and gaming PCs. We carry common parts, so most repairs are done in one visit.

  • Won't start, crashes, or runs slow
  • Cracked screens and failed batteries
  • SSD and memory upgrades
  • Windows and macOS reinstalls

Virus & malware removal

Full clean-up, then protection set up properly so it doesn't come back a fortnight later.

  • Ransomware and spyware removal
  • Browser hijacks and pop-ups
  • Scam and remote-access recovery
  • Ongoing protection configured

Data recovery

Failed drives, dead SSDs and deleted files. Free assessment first — we'll tell you the odds before you commit.

  • Hard drive and SSD recovery
  • Accidentally deleted files
  • Corrupted or unreadable storage
  • Backup set up so it can't recur

Wi-Fi & networks

The most common callout we get. Usually it's coverage, not your internet plan.

  • Dead zones and dropouts
  • Mesh Wi-Fi installed properly
  • NBN faults diagnosed
  • Small business networks
